Healthcare that comes home.

Mirha Vitals Home Healthcare LLC is a 24/7 DHA-licensed home healthcare provider in Dubai, bringing professional medical and nursing care to homes, hotels, villas and workplaces.

Our Vision

To provide trusted, safe, professional and compassionate healthcare to patients wherever they are, 24/7

Our Mission

To deliver accessible, patient-centred healthcare through qualified healthcare professional, bringing medical and nursing services directly to the patient's home.

Our Credentials

Licensed. Regulated. Accountable.

All services will be provided within the facility's approved DHA scope and by appropriately licensed healthcare professionals.

Our doctors are DHA-licensed healthcare professionals who have successfully completed DHA DataFlow verification and the required DHA examination.

  • DHA-licensed healthcare professionals
  • Supervised by a qualified Medical Director
  • Confidential, documented care

Standards

Clinical Governance

The facility will operate under the supervision of a qualified Medical Director. Mirha Vitals will maintain appropriate policies for:

  • Patient safety
  • Infection prevention and control
  • Consent
  • Clinical documentation
  • Medication management
  • Emergency escalation
  • Incident reporting
  • Patient confidentiality
  • Quality improvement

Founder & Medical Director

Nazia Javed

Founder & Medical Director

With 17 years devoted to healthcare, Nazia Javed founded Mirha Vitals on a simple, heartfelt belief: Care should never feel cold or distant. It should feel personal, compassionate, and within everyone's reach. Having stood beside countless patients and families in their most vulnerable moments, she understands that true healing begins where we feel safest, surrounded by familiar faces, comforting walls, and the warmth of home. That is why she built Mirha Vitals: to bring expert, dignified medical care to your doorstep, so you are never just a patient, but a person truly seen, heard, and cared for.
