1. Overview

Mirha Vitals Home Healthcare LLC ("Mirha Vitals", "we", "us") provides home healthcare services in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. Our registered office is at 205 - GBH Business Center, Al Rasheed Road, Al Muteena, Dubai, UAE.

We respect the privacy of everyone whose information we handle, including:

  • visitors to this website;
  • people who contact us to ask about or arrange care;
  • our patients;
  • family members, carers and other authorised representatives who act for a patient.

This policy explains what information we handle, why we handle it, who we may share it with, how we protect it and the choices and rights you have. It applies to information we process through this website and through our phone, WhatsApp and email channels, and to information we hold in connection with the care we provide.

This policy does not replace any consent form or information you are given at the time of care. Where a specific consent or notice applies to a particular service, that consent or notice also applies.

2. Information we collect

What we collect depends on how you interact with us. Browsing this website or asking a general question does not require you to share any medical information.

2.1 Visiting this website

This website does not contain forms, accounts or log-ins, and you do not need to give us any personal information to browse it. Like most websites, when your browser loads our pages, technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information and the pages requested is processed by our website host and by the third-party services described in section 8.

2.2 Enquiries and appointment requests

When you call us, message us on WhatsApp or email us, we may collect:

  • your name and contact details, such as your phone number, WhatsApp number and email address;
  • your preferred way of being contacted;
  • the service you are asking about, your preferred date and time, and the location where care is needed (for example a home, hotel or workplace address);
  • the reason for your request or the symptoms you choose to describe, so that we can arrange the right care;
  • the content of your messages and any information you choose to send us.

2.3 Patients

When we provide care, our healthcare professionals collect and record the health information needed to assess, treat and follow up safely. Depending on the service, this may include:

  • identification details, date of birth and contact details;
  • relevant medical history, current symptoms, medications and allergies;
  • clinical assessments, observations, vital signs, diagnoses, treatment and care plans, and progress notes;
  • laboratory samples and test results, reports and records from other healthcare providers, where needed for your care;
  • referral information and consent records;
  • health insurance details, where applicable.

2.4 Authorised representatives

If you contact us or make decisions on behalf of a patient, such as a parent, guardian, family member or carer, we may collect your name, contact details and your relationship to the patient.

2.5 Billing and payment

Where applicable, we collect the information needed to invoice and receive payment for services, such as billing name, invoice details and payment records. This website does not take online payments.

3. How we collect information

We collect information:

  • directly from you, when you call us, message us on WhatsApp, email us or speak to our team;
  • during care, when our licensed healthcare professionals assess and treat you and record what they find;
  • from your authorised representative, when someone contacts us or acts on your behalf;
  • from other parties involved in your care, such as laboratories that analyse samples we collect, other healthcare providers who refer you or share relevant records, and health insurers, where this applies and is permitted by law;
  • automatically, through the website technologies described in section 8.

4. Why we use information

We use personal information only for purposes connected with our services and our legal duties, including to:

  • respond to your enquiries;
  • schedule, dispatch and coordinate home healthcare visits and teleconsultations;
  • confirm your identity and relevant details where this is necessary;
  • assess, provide, coordinate, document and follow up on your care, including coordination between our doctors and nurses and referral to other providers where needed;
  • send appointment confirmations, reminders and service updates;
  • invoice, process payments, deal with insurers where applicable and keep required business records;
  • maintain the quality and safety of our services and handle feedback and complaints;
  • keep this website secure and working properly;
  • meet our legal, regulatory and healthcare obligations;
  • send promotional messages, only where the law permits and you have agreed where agreement is required.

We do not use your personal information for purposes unrelated to the purposes it was collected for unless we have a lawful basis to do so. We do not use your health information for non-healthcare purposes without your written approval, except where the law allows this (see section 6).

5. Legal basis for processing

The legal basis for using your information depends on the type of information, the purpose and the law that applies to it. In the UAE, personal data is governed by Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data, and health data is subject to specific legislation, including Federal Law No. 2 of 2019 on the Use of Information and Communication Technology in Health Fields and the requirements of the Dubai Health Authority (DHA).

Depending on the circumstances, we rely on one or more of the following:

  • Healthcare provision: processing needed for medical assessment, diagnosis, treatment and the management of health services;
  • Your consent: for example, before we collect and record your health information where consent is required, before we share it for purposes other than your direct care where the law requires consent, and before we send promotional messages where consent is required;
  • Contract: processing needed to arrange and deliver the services you ask for;
  • Legal obligation: processing needed to comply with UAE and Dubai laws and regulatory requirements, including record-keeping duties;
  • Vital interests and public health: processing needed to protect your health or safety or that of others, or to protect public health;
  • Legal claims: processing needed to establish, exercise or defend legal rights.

Contacting us with an enquiry does not by itself mean you agree to receive marketing, and it does not authorise us to use your health information for any purpose other than responding to you and arranging your care.

6. Health information and patient confidentiality

Health information is particularly sensitive. We keep it confidential and allow access to it only where this is authorised.

  • Access is limited to authorised members of our team, such as the doctors, nurses and coordinators involved in your care, and only to the extent they need it to do their work.
  • Our healthcare professionals and staff are bound by professional and contractual confidentiality obligations.
  • We handle health information in line with applicable UAE health legislation and DHA policies on health data protection, confidentiality and information sharing.

We do not disclose your health information to unauthorised persons. UAE law does, however, permit or require disclosure without your written approval in certain cases. These include, as set out in Federal Law No. 2 of 2019:

  • information required by health insurance companies or other entities that fund your healthcare;
  • scientific and clinical research, provided your identity is not disclosed;
  • preventive and curative measures to protect public health, or to protect your health and safety or that of people connected with you;
  • requests from competent judicial authorities;
  • requests from the health authority for the purposes of control, inspection and protection of public health.

Any such disclosure is limited to what is required and handled under the applicable rules. We cannot promise absolute confidentiality where the law requires or permits disclosure.

7. WhatsApp, phone and email

You can contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email. Please keep in mind:

  • WhatsApp is operated by WhatsApp LLC (part of Meta). Messages you send us through WhatsApp are processed by WhatsApp under its own terms of service and privacy policy. A WhatsApp conversation is not automatically part of your medical record. Where information from a conversation is clinically relevant, we may record it in your patient record.
  • Email may pass through third-party systems, and we cannot guarantee its security in transit.
  • Phone calls are handled by our team.

To make a booking, you only need to share the details needed to arrange care. Please avoid sending detailed medical documents or images unless our team asks for them, and ask us if you would prefer a different way to share sensitive information.

8. Cookies and website technologies

At the date of this policy, this website does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

8.1 Browser storage

The website stores two small values in your browser's session storage. They remember whether the opening animation has already played and smooth the transition between pages. They do not identify you, are not sent to us and are deleted when you close the browser tab.

9. How we share information

We share personal information only where needed for a specific purpose and where the law allows, with:

  • authorised healthcare professionals involved in your care;
  • other healthcare providers and facilities, such as laboratories, hospitals and specialists, where needed for your care or referral and permitted by law;
  • health insurers or other funders of your care, where applicable;
  • service providers who support our operations, such as website hosting, IT and security, communication, scheduling and payment providers, who may only use the information to provide their services to us;
  • the Dubai Health Authority, other regulators, courts and public authorities, where disclosure is required or permitted by law.

We expect our service providers to protect your information and keep it confidential. We do not sell personal information.

10. International data transfers

UAE law places strict controls on health data. Under Federal Law No. 2 of 2019, health data relating to health services provided in the UAE may not be stored, processed or transferred outside the UAE except in the cases permitted by the competent authorities. We store and handle health information in line with these requirements.

Other personal information, such as messages sent through WhatsApp or technical data received by the providers in section 8, may be processed by those providers outside the UAE under their own arrangements. Where we transfer personal information outside the UAE ourselves, we do so only where the applicable legal requirements and safeguards are met.

11. Data security

We use technical and organisational measures suited to the sensitivity of the information we hold, which may include:

  • role-based access, so team members see only what they need;
  • confidentiality obligations for staff and service providers;
  • secure handling, storage and disposal of paper and electronic records;
  • secure connections for the website.

No method of transmitting or storing information over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach affects your information, we will act in line with our legal and regulatory obligations, including notifying you and the relevant authorities where required.

12. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as long as the law requires.

  • Medical records: UAE law requires health data to be kept for no less than 25 years from the date of the last health procedure provided to the patient (Federal Law No. 2 of 2019, Article 20). Longer periods may apply under other rules.
  • Enquiries and messages that do not lead to care: kept only as long as needed to respond and for related record-keeping.
  • Billing and business records: kept for the periods required by applicable financial and commercial laws.

When information is no longer needed, we delete or securely destroy it, or anonymise it so that it can no longer identify you.

13. Your privacy rights

Depending on the applicable law and your circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • be informed about how your information is processed;
  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete information corrected;
  • ask us to erase your information, where the law allows;
  • ask us to restrict or stop certain processing;
  • object to processing for direct marketing at any time;
  • receive your information in a structured, machine-readable format, where applicable;
  • withdraw your consent where we rely on it, without affecting processing already carried out;
  • make a complaint to the competent authority.

These rights have limits. In particular, we must keep medical records for the periods required by law, so we cannot erase them on request, although we can correct inaccurate entries. We may also decline or limit a request where the law permits.

13.1 How to make a request

Email us at info@mirhavitals.com with the subject line "Privacy request", or contact us using the details in section 17. We may need to verify your identity, or your authority to act for someone else, before we respond. We will reply within the time required by applicable law and regulation.

14. Children's information

We provide care to children only in line with applicable UAE law and consent requirements. Information about a child is normally provided by, and requests about it handled through, a parent, legal guardian or other authorised representative. Our team will explain who can give consent and receive information for a child when care is arranged.

15. Third-party websites and services

This website contains links to third-party websites and platforms, such as WhatsApp, Instagram, LinkedIn and Google Maps. Those services have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their policies before giving them any personal information.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our services, our website, the technology we use or the law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Where a change materially affects how we use your information, we will take reasonable steps to let you know.
