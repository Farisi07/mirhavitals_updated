IV Therapy at Home

IV therapy given at home by licensed professionals.

Overview

About this service

IV Therapy at Home is provided by licensed healthcare professionals in the comfort of your own surroundings, following an appropriate clinical assessment.

Your comfort and safety are looked after throughout the visit, with careful attention to infection control and medication management.

What's included

  • Clinical assessment before therapy begins
  • IV therapy administered by licensed healthcare professionals
  • Observation during the session
  • Attention to infection control and medication management
  • Clinical documentation and follow-up guidance

Who it's for

  • Individuals and families
  • Patients requiring nursing care
  • Working professionals
  • Hotel guests and visitors

How to book

  1. Call or WhatsApp Mirha Vitals.

  2. Give your name, location, phone number, and symptoms/reason for consultation.

  3. Mirha Vitals will confirm the doctor and estimated arrival time.
