Home healthcare services in Dubai

Medical, nursing and supportive care, arranged through a single call or WhatsApp message and delivered wherever you are in Dubai.

01

Doctor Teleconsultation

Talk through your symptoms with a DHA-licensed doctor by phone or video, from wherever you are. The doctor will advise on the next step, including a home visit or referral where that is the right course.

Explore Doctor Teleconsultation Book Your Appointment

02

Doctor on Call / Home Visit

A DHA-licensed doctor comes to your home, hotel, villa or workplace to assess you in person. It is a convenient alternative to visiting a clinic for many common medical concerns.

Explore Doctor on Call / Home Visit Book Your Appointment

03

Nurse on Call / Home Nursing

Licensed nurses provide skilled nursing care in your own home, working in coordination with the doctor. Care is planned around the needs of each patient and family.

Explore Nurse on Call / Home Nursing Book Your Appointment

04

Laboratory Sample Collection at Home

A licensed healthcare professional visits you to collect laboratory samples at home, so there is no need to travel to a clinic or wait in a queue.

Explore Laboratory Sample Collection at Home Book Your Appointment

05

IV Therapy at Home

IV therapy is administered at home by licensed healthcare professionals, following assessment and within the facility's approved DHA scope.

Explore IV Therapy at Home Book Your Appointment

06

Wound Care at Home

Licensed nurses assess and dress wounds at home, with close attention to hygiene and comfort. It is especially convenient for patients recovering after a procedure.

Explore Wound Care at Home Book Your Appointment

07

Elderly Care at Home

Compassionate, patient-centred care for older adults in their own home, coordinated between doctor and nurse and planned around each person's needs.

Explore Elderly Care at Home Book Your Appointment

All services will be provided within the facility's approved DHA scope and by appropriately licensed healthcare professionals.
