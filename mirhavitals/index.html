Frequently Asked Questions

A Doctor On Call service allows you to receive professional medical consultation and care at your home, hotel, office or preferred location in Dubai without visiting a clinic.

  1. Call or WhatsApp Mirha Vitals.
  2. Give your name, location, phone number, and symptoms/reason for consultation.
  3. Mirha Vitals will confirm the doctor and estimated arrival time.

Our doctors are DHA-licensed healthcare professionals who have successfully completed DHA DataFlow verification and the required DHA examination.

Yes, Mirha Vitals healthcare professionals are available 24/7 to your home, hotel, villa, or office - whenever you need it.

A Doctor On Call can assess many common medical concerns, including general consultations, acute and chronic pain, fever, flu, bacterial, viral and fungal infections, and other acute emergency healthcare needs. The doctor will evaluate your condition, recommend appropriate treatment and give a referral according to your problem.
